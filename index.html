Who are we:

We are the Skyline Coding Club! This is a place where skyline students can share their passion for coding. Most importantly, we also provide opportunities for our members to learn and show their skills.

We present opportunities and participate as a group in local hackathons. The most recent one we went to was Hack Club Haven!

Mini hackathons:

We host mini hackathons for our members to compete in. We teach them what they need to know to compete and help them do it. See more on this page.
