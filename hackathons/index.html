Mini Hackathons

Every trimester we have a mini-hackathon, where members can work on projects and compete for prizes.


Hackathons:

2026 Trimester 1 Website-Making Hackathon

We are currently hosting a mini Website-Making hackathon, where members can create a website and then submit it. Along with this, we are teaching people who are unfamiliar with web design with some basic skills in order to create a website to submit.

Rules:

You can create any website that you want to submit. You are also allowed to submit a website that you have made in the past. It is due by the end of Trimester 1, when they will be graded based off of both how good they look, and how cool they are in general. At the end we will showcase the websites everyone has created as well. The winner will win a domain that they choose for one year.
